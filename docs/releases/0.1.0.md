# noface 0.1.0

The first release. noface replaces the Kindle Paperwhite's home screen and reader with
one built for speed: a new page in about 60 ms, against about 330 ms in the stock
reader, with your Amazon library, sync, highlights and Wi‑Fi still working.

**For the Kindle Paperwhite, 12th generation (2024), jailbroken, only.**

## What's in it

- **Your library and nothing else:** your books, a continue-reading shelf, filters,
  sorting, search and reading stats, with no store shelves or banners.
- **EPUBs that just open,** copied over USB, beside your Amazon books.
- **A fast reader:** four typefaces plus your own, size, spacing, margins, bold word
  starts, chapters, Go To, search, pictures full screen with zoom.
- **Definitions** that download themselves the first time you hold a word, in English
  and Portuguese, and **highlights** that sync with the Kindle apps.
- **Series** grouped from your own files, kept in order, the next one offered when you
  finish a book.
- **Running mode,** for reading on a treadmill: a sentence or so at a time, large and
  still, moving on at a pace learned from your taps, with big tap areas and a pause
  you can see from a stride away. Aa > Themes > Running.
- **Wi‑Fi off while you read,** and an estimate of how long your charge will last.
- **Chinese characters** in an English book drawn in the Kindle's own Chinese
  typeface, rather than as empty boxes.
