# noface 0.1.1

Fixes for things you'd touch every day, and a much bigger test suite behind them: every
control is now tapped and checked on a real Kindle before a release.

**For the Kindle Paperwhite, 12th generation (2024), jailbroken, only.**

## Fixed

- **Aa sheet:** the tabs and both rows of typefaces take a full fingertip of taps, where
  the second row and the tabs were a little short. The typeface grid stays on the page
  you turned to when you change the text size or Bold, rather than jumping back.
- **Search and Go to page:** a tap just above the field no longer closes the sheet and
  loses the results; a long query shows its end, so what you're typing stays in view;
  and typing in Go to page no longer whitens the top of the keyboard's tray.
- **Brightness and warmth:** the level number is redrawn whole when it changes, where
  the tops of the old digits could stay behind.
- **Library:** the unchosen filter tabs and the hero's quiet lines fade just their text,
  no longer graying part of a tab's outline.
- **Memory and pages:** a book's search lets go of its results once they're shown, and
  a page that fails to draw is never shown as if it had, with half of another page in it.
- **Layout details:** the toolbar's Aa label and running mode's phrase are centred,
  where they sat 16 and 12 pixels off, and the picture viewer's close button is a full
  fingertip across.
