# noface 0.2.0

A full keyboard, a quick panel that never sends you out of noface by surprise, and
reading that's quicker on the Kindle: search and bold word starts are over twice as
fast, hyphenation two to three times. Letters in every script are now read the way
Unicode says, through noface's first outside library.

## New

- **The keyboard is laid out as the Kindle's own.** Shift for a capital (tap it twice
  to lock capitals), 123 for digits and the common symbols, #+= for the rest, with €,
  £ and ¥. Hold a letter to see its accented forms (é, ç, ã, ü, ø…) and tap one. Two
  thumbs typing fast type both keys. Go to page opens on the digits, and a series
  name typed with capitals is kept as you typed it.
- **Wi-Fi turns on and off from the quick panel**, without leaving noface: the tile
  is airplane mode's other side, and shows the name of the network you're connected
  to. The Kindle tile is now the panel's only way back to the Kindle's own screens.
- **Wi-Fi paused while reading is shown as paused.** A couple of minutes into a book
  noface turns the radio off to save the battery; the panel now says so ("Paused")
  instead of showing airplane mode as if you'd turned it on, and one tap on Wi-Fi
  brings it back for the rest of the book.
- **Turning Wi-Fi off with Bluetooth on asks first**, as airplane mode turns
  Bluetooth off too.

## Better

- **Faster on the Kindle:** searching a book is over twice as fast, laying out bold
  word starts nearly three times, and hyphenation two and a half to three and a half
  times, with pages breaking exactly where they did.
- **Every script:** search, the dictionary and bold word starts know letters, capitals
  and accents in Greek, Cyrillic and every other script, not only Latin ones, and
  typed quotes find a book's curly ones ("don't" finds "don’t").
- **Dictionaries download from inside noface**: the "Get dictionary" button works,
  and the dictionaries are rebuilt to match how noface now looks words up.
- **Series names** match whatever the case of their accented letters, so "Les âmes"
  typed and "Les Âmes" from a book are one series.

## Fixed

- Text with emoji or variation sequences (❤️) no longer gets a box drawn over it.
- A long search with accented letters stays inside its field.
- A book with footnote marks in another script (Cyrillic, Japanese) lays out again
  as it opens, so no text is skipped on pages laid out by 0.1.2.

## For anyone reporting a crash

noface now writes where a crash happened to `noface.log`, and each release carries
`noface-<version>-<model>-symbols` beside its zip, which turns those lines into the
places in the code (see "Reading a crash" in docs/DEVELOPING.md). Attaching
`noface.log` to a report is enough.
