# noface 0.4.0

Your place follows you between noface, the Kindle's own reader, the Kindle apps and
your other devices, both ways, and noface answers sooner: nothing it can do after a
screen is shown is done before it any more.

## New

- **Your place, kept in step.** On books sent to your Kindle, the place you leave in
  noface is where the Kindle apps and your other devices open, and noface opens where
  the Kindle's own reader left off. Read on your phone since, and noface asks whether
  to go there as the book opens ("Go to 33%? Last read on Phone"), as the Kindle does.
- **The library knows too.** A book read further on another device shows how far you
  got there in the library, without opening it. It's checked again each time the
  Kindle wakes.
- **One tile to turn it off.** The pull-down panel has a tile for keeping your place in
  step with your other devices. With it off, noface asks the Kindle nothing about a
  book's place and tells it nothing.

## Better

- **Waking is instant.** The library or your page is back as soon as you unlock, with
  the clock right; the battery, Wi‑Fi and book list are read just after, where waking
  waited about half a second for them.
- **The toolbar answers at once.** Its first tap no longer waits for the Kindle's
  status, and closing it brings the page and its clock back in one refresh.
- **Settings change faster.** A new typeface or size shows straight away and is saved
  after; choices in More, and closing a card, take one refresh instead of two.
- **Leaving a book is quicker.** The library shows first; your place is passed on,
  Wi‑Fi comes back and the sync is asked for just after.
- **Page turns never pause to save.** Your reading speed and the day's reading are
  saved when you lock or close the book, not every few pages.

## Fixed

- Books you copied onto the Kindle have their highlights and place filed as yours but
  not bought, as the Kindle's own reader files them.
- Marking a book unread starts it over on the Kindle and at Amazon too, so an older
  place from the Kindle's own reader no longer comes back.
- The theme in use has its tick drawn whole, the Running button's label sits inside
  it, and Text settings or Stop from running's pause card refresh the screen once.

## Updating from 0.3.0

Copy the new **noface** folder over the old one, as always. On its first start noface
builds its library index again, once. A book shows another device's place in the
library after you've opened it once in this version. Your places, notes and stats are
kept; backing up the **noface-data** folder first costs nothing.
