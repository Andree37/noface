# noface 0.4.1

Books you send to your Kindle show up by themselves, a book in the cloud you no longer
want can be hidden, and Sync says when it's done.

## New

- **Hide a book in the cloud.** Hold a book that isn't on the Kindle, and its card offers
  to hide it from noface's library. It stays in your Amazon library and on your other
  devices; to delete it from Amazon, use the Kindle's own screens. A hidden book shows
  again once it's on the Kindle.

## Better

- **Sent books appear without waking the Kindle.** The library notices new books in the
  Kindle's catalog by itself, and watches for them closely for a few minutes after you
  tap Sync. A book stuck as downloading no longer keeps new ones from showing.
- **Sync turns back** from "Syncing" once the Kindle says the sync is done, as its own
  button does.
- **Your place is always kept in step** with the Kindle and your other devices. The
  panel's tile for it is gone, as is the setting.

## Fixed

- A tap made just as the library redraws itself is no longer taken on the redrawn
  screen, where a different book could sit under it.
- A download that finished while the Kindle still listed it as coming shows as here.
- noface could stop when the Kindle wrote its catalog at the moment noface looked at it
  after waking.

## Updating from 0.4.0

Copy the new **noface** folder over the old one, as always. On its first start noface
builds its library index again, once. If you had turned keeping your place in step off,
it's on again now. Your places, notes and stats are kept.
