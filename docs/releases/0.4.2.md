# noface 0.4.2

Books you send to your Kindle keep arriving.

## Fixed

- **Sent books keep arriving.** The Kindle's own service that adds new books
  from your Amazon library can stop when another of its services restarts, and the
  Kindle only starts it again at its next boot, so sent books stop arriving, in noface
  and in the Kindle's own screens alike. Every sync noface asks for (tapping Sync,
  closing a book, saving a highlight) now starts it again first if it had stopped, and
  waits for it to answer.

## Updating from 0.4.1

Copy the new **noface** folder over the old one, as always. Your places, notes and
stats are kept.
